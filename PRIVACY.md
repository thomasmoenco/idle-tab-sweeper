# Privacy policy — Idle Tab Sweeper

Idle Tab Sweeper does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. To know how long a tab has been unused, it stores the addresses of your open tabs and the time each was last used in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Entries are deleted within a minute after the tab is closed. Your day settings are stored with Chrome's own settings sync. The extension makes no network requests.
